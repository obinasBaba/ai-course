# aie-weekly

Keep the course alive. Short, honest, forward-looking.

## Procedure

1. **Gather** from `progress.md` (Session log for the last 7 days, Lab metrics, Quiz history, Modules) and `roadmap.md` (this week's plan) and `cert/ai-103.md`.

2. **Show** (compact):
   ```
   Week N (dates) · Planned Xh · Actual Yh · Sessions Z (min-days included)
   Moved: <module/lab progress>
   New numbers: <metrics logged this week>
   Reviews due next week: <modules>
   AI-103 readiness: A% (Δ) · Roadmap: on track | +N weeks behind
   ```

3. **Ask three questions** (one message):
   - What got in the way?
   - What worked?
   - Realistic hours next week?

4. **Re-plan.** Adjust next week in `roadmap.md` → "Current week plan". If behind: **move dates, don't cut retention mechanics**. Use buffer weeks first; then trim stretch goals; only then shift milestones (and say which). Never silently drop a module.

5. **Momentum check.** No public `labs/` commit in 14+ days → propose the smallest publishable thing (README metric table, a chart, a short write-up).

6. **Log** a row in `progress.md` → Weekly retros: `week | planned h | actual h | moved | blocker | next-week focus`.

## Style
No guilt, no pep talk. Facts → one adjustment → done.
